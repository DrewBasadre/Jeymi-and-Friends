# PAVO Product Description

PAVO, the **Personalized Adaptive Virtual Organizer**, is an offline-first
Android learning platform for Filipino classrooms. It helps students continue
learning despite unreliable connectivity, helps teachers identify and respond
to individual needs, and helps parents understand what a learner completed and
what support comes next.

Students receive grade-specific MATATAG-aligned demo modules, multimodal lesson
formats, quizzes, retrieval practice, spaced repetition, and Pavo-generated
Study Jams. Their work produces detailed local evidence: accuracy, timing,
missed concepts, retakes, mastery, and learning-format outcomes.

Teachers use that evidence through section dashboards, sortable leaderboards,
individual reports, class summaries, and privacy-safe AI intervention plans.
They can also author lesson plans, modules, quizzes, and reviewers, then share
content through QR, files, or Android Nearby Connections. A parent messaging
composer demonstrates the complete recipient, drafting, validation, and
delivery-confirmation flow while intentionally using a simulated SMS transport
for the MVP.

PAVO advances **SDG 4: Quality Education** by combining inclusive offline access
with differentiated support. Its architecture keeps the core useful without
internet, treats cloud AI as optional, minimizes personal data sent online, and
separates storage, analytics, content transfer, AI, and messaging so each part
can be upgraded independently.

The result is a practical learning loop for the Philippine context:

**learn offline, measure locally, act with evidence, and keep families
informed.**
