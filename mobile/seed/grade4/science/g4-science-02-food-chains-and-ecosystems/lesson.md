# Food Chains and Ecosystems

> WAIS demo content aligned to MATATAG Quarter 1 learning goals. This is an original classroom learning aid, not official curriculum text.

## Learning goal

By the end of this lesson, you can explain **food chain**, use it in a clear example, and apply the idea in a short task.

![Concept guide for Food Chains and Ecosystems](images/concept-guide.webp)

## Key idea

`food chain` is the focus of this lesson. Energy moves from the Sun to producers and then to consumers and decomposers.

Connect the example to the key idea, then explain the connection in your own words.

## Worked example

Grass feeds a grasshopper, which may feed a frog.

Notice how the example supports the key idea instead of simply naming it. Ask: Which detail is evidence for the main idea, and how do you know?

![Worked example for Food Chains and Ecosystems](images/worked-example.webp)

## Guided practice

1. Restate the key idea in your own words.
2. Identify the important information in the worked example.
3. Complete this task: Build a local food chain and predict one population change.
4. Check your answer by returning to the definition and visual.

## Talk and think

- What detail was most useful?
- What is one common mistake someone might make?
- Where could you observe or use this idea at home, in school, or in the community?

## Remember

The goal is not only to memorize **food chain**. You should be able to recognize it, explain it, and use it in a new situation.
