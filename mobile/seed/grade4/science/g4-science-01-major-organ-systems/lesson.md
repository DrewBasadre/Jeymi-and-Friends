# Major Organ Systems

> PAVO demo content aligned to MATATAG Quarter 1 learning goals. This is an original classroom learning aid, not official curriculum text.

## Learning goal

By the end of this lesson, you can explain **organ systems**, use it in a clear example, and apply the idea in a short task.

![Concept guide for Major Organ Systems](images/concept-guide.webp)

## Key idea

`organ systems` is the focus of this lesson. Digestive, respiratory, circulatory, skeletal, and muscular systems interact to support the body.

Connect the example to the key idea, then explain the connection in your own words.

## Worked example

Muscles pull on bones while blood supplies working cells with oxygen.

Notice how the example supports the key idea instead of simply naming it. Ask: Which detail is evidence for the main idea, and how do you know?

![Worked example for Major Organ Systems](images/worked-example.webp)

## Guided practice

1. Restate the key idea in your own words.
2. Identify the important information in the worked example.
3. Complete this task: Make a flow diagram connecting three body systems during exercise.
4. Check your answer by returning to the definition and visual.

## Talk and think

- What detail was most useful?
- What is one common mistake someone might make?
- Where could you observe or use this idea at home, in school, or in the community?

## Remember

The goal is not only to memorize **organ systems**. You should be able to recognize it, explain it, and use it in a new situation.
