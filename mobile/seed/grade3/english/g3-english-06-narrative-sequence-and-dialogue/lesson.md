# Narrative Sequence and Dialogue

> PAVO demo content aligned to MATATAG Quarter 1 learning goals. This is an original classroom learning aid, not official curriculum text.

## Learning goal

By the end of this lesson, you can explain **narrative**, use it in a clear example, and apply the idea in a short task.

![Concept guide for Narrative Sequence and Dialogue](images/concept-guide.webp)

## Key idea

`narrative` is the focus of this lesson. Narratives organize events and use dialogue to reveal character and action.

Connect the example to the key idea, then explain the connection in your own words.

## Worked example

Quotation marks show the exact words a character speaks.

Notice how the example supports the key idea instead of simply naming it. Ask: What changed? What stayed the same?

![Worked example for Narrative Sequence and Dialogue](images/worked-example.webp)

## Guided practice

1. Restate the key idea in your own words.
2. Identify the important information in the worked example.
3. Complete this task: Write a short beginning, problem, and ending with dialogue.
4. Check your answer by returning to the definition and visual.

## Talk and think

- What detail was most useful?
- What is one common mistake someone might make?
- Where could you observe or use this idea at home, in school, or in the community?

## Remember

The goal is not only to memorize **narrative**. You should be able to recognize it, explain it, and use it in a new situation.
