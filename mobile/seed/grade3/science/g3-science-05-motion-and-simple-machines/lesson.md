# Motion and Simple Machines

> WAIS demo content aligned to MATATAG Quarter 1 learning goals. This is an original classroom learning aid, not official curriculum text.

## Learning goal

By the end of this lesson, you can explain **motion**, use it in a clear example, and apply the idea in a short task.

![Concept guide for Motion and Simple Machines](images/concept-guide.webp)

## Key idea

`motion` is the focus of this lesson. Position changes over time, and simple machines can make work easier.

Connect the example to the key idea, then explain the connection in your own words.

## Worked example

A ramp lets a box move upward with less lifting force.

Notice how the example supports the key idea instead of simply naming it. Ask: What changed? What stayed the same?

![Worked example for Motion and Simple Machines](images/worked-example.webp)

## Guided practice

1. Restate the key idea in your own words.
2. Identify the important information in the worked example.
3. Complete this task: Compare moving an object with and without a ramp.
4. Check your answer by returning to the definition and visual.

## Talk and think

- What detail was most useful?
- What is one common mistake someone might make?
- Where could you observe or use this idea at home, in school, or in the community?

## Remember

The goal is not only to memorize **motion**. You should be able to recognize it, explain it, and use it in a new situation.
