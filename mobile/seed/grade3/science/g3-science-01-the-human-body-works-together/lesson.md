# The Human Body Works Together

> PAVO demo content aligned to MATATAG Quarter 1 learning goals. This is an original classroom learning aid, not official curriculum text.

## Learning goal

By the end of this lesson, you can explain **body systems**, use it in a clear example, and apply the idea in a short task.

![Concept guide for The Human Body Works Together](images/concept-guide.webp)

## Key idea

`body systems` is the focus of this lesson. Groups of organs work together to move materials, sense changes, and support life.

Connect the example to the key idea, then explain the connection in your own words.

## Worked example

The lungs bring oxygen in while blood carries it around the body.

Notice how the example supports the key idea instead of simply naming it. Ask: What changed? What stayed the same?

![Worked example for The Human Body Works Together](images/worked-example.webp)

## Guided practice

1. Restate the key idea in your own words.
2. Identify the important information in the worked example.
3. Complete this task: Trace the path of air from the nose to the lungs.
4. Check your answer by returning to the definition and visual.

## Talk and think

- What detail was most useful?
- What is one common mistake someone might make?
- Where could you observe or use this idea at home, in school, or in the community?

## Remember

The goal is not only to memorize **body systems**. You should be able to recognize it, explain it, and use it in a new situation.
