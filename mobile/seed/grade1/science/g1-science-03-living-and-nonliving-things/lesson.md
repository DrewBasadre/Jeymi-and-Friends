# Living and Nonliving Things

> WAIS demo content aligned to MATATAG Quarter 1 learning goals. This is an original classroom learning aid, not official curriculum text.

## Learning goal

By the end of this lesson, you can explain **living things**, use it in a clear example, and apply the idea in a short task.

![Concept guide for Living and Nonliving Things](images/concept-guide.webp)

## Key idea

`living things` is the focus of this lesson. Living things grow and need air, water, and food; nonliving things do not grow.

Say the key words aloud. Point to the visual as you explain what you notice.

## Worked example

A seedling grows taller, but a stone stays the same size.

Notice how the example supports the key idea instead of simply naming it. Ask: What changed? What stayed the same?

![Worked example for Living and Nonliving Things](images/worked-example.webp)

## Guided practice

1. Restate the key idea in your own words.
2. Identify the important information in the worked example.
3. Complete this task: Sort six nearby objects into living and nonliving groups.
4. Check your answer by returning to the definition and visual.

## Talk and think

- What detail was most useful?
- What is one common mistake someone might make?
- Where could you observe or use this idea at home, in school, or in the community?

## Remember

The goal is not only to memorize **living things**. You should be able to recognize it, explain it, and use it in a new situation.
