# Beginning, Middle, and End

> WAIS demo content aligned to MATATAG Quarter 1 learning goals. This is an original classroom learning aid, not official curriculum text.

## Learning goal

By the end of this lesson, you can explain **story sequence**, use it in a clear example, and apply the idea in a short task.

![Concept guide for Beginning, Middle, and End](images/concept-guide.webp)

## Key idea

`story sequence` is the focus of this lesson. Events in a story happen in an order: beginning, middle, and end.

Say the key words aloud. Point to the visual as you explain what you notice.

## Worked example

First Ana plants a seed, next she waters it, and finally a sprout appears.

Notice how the example supports the key idea instead of simply naming it. Ask: What changed? What stayed the same?

![Worked example for Beginning, Middle, and End](images/worked-example.webp)

## Guided practice

1. Restate the key idea in your own words.
2. Identify the important information in the worked example.
3. Complete this task: Retell a short story using first, next, and last.
4. Check your answer by returning to the definition and visual.

## Talk and think

- What detail was most useful?
- What is one common mistake someone might make?
- Where could you observe or use this idea at home, in school, or in the community?

## Remember

The goal is not only to memorize **story sequence**. You should be able to recognize it, explain it, and use it in a new situation.
